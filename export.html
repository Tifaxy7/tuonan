FAQs

What is RORO shipping?

RORO means Roll-On/Roll-Off — vehicles are driven directly onto the vessel, making it a cost-effective method for shipping buses.

How does container shipping work?

Your bus is secured inside a shipping container, protecting it fully during transit. Ideal for smaller buses and minibuses.

When should I choose bulk shipment?

Bulk shipment is ideal when transporting multiple buses together — it reduces per-unit cost and streamlines delivery logistics.

Are there size or weight limits?

Yes, each shipping method has specific size and weight restrictions. We'll advise the best method based on your bus specifications.

Is insurance included?

Insurance options are available to protect your shipment. We can arrange marine cargo insurance upon request.

How long does shipping usually take?

Shipping times vary by method and destination but typically range from 2 to 6 weeks depending on your target port.
