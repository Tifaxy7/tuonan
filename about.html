About Tuonan

Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Quality

Tuonan Bus Export is based in Anhui, China — one of the country's leading regions for commercial vehicle manufacturing. We specialize in exporting new and certified used commercial buses to global markets, with a strong focus on Africa.

Our deep connections with China's top bus manufacturers allow us to offer a wide selection of vehicles at competitive prices, without compromising on quality or reliability.

We understand the unique transport needs of African markets and have built our business around delivering solutions that work — from the right bus model to seamless shipping and after-sales support.
