The Process

How We Export Your Buses

01

Inquiry & Quote

Contact us with your bus requirements — type, quantity, budget, and destination. We'll respond with a detailed quote within 24 hours.

02

Selection & Agreement

We present available buses matching your requirements with full details, photos, and pricing. Once you're satisfied, we formalize the agreement.

03

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every bus undergoes a thorough inspection before shipment. We can also arrange third-party inspection for your peace of mind.

04

Documentation

We handle all export documentation including the Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Commercial Invoice, and Packing List.

05

Shipping

Buses are loaded and shipped via trusted freight partners. We coordinate with shipping lines for the most efficient routes to your port.

06

Delivery & Support

We track your shipment and keep you updated throughout. Once delivered, our after-sales support team is always available.

Documentation We Handle

Full Export Compliance

📄

Bill of Lading

Official shipping document confirming receipt of cargo and terms of transport to your destination port.

🏷️

Certificate of Origin

Certified document confirming the buses are manufactured in China, required for customs clearance in most countries.

💼

Commercial Invoice

Detailed invoice listing the buses, quantities, values, and transaction terms for customs and payment purposes.

📦

Packing List

Comprehensive list of all items in the shipment including spare parts and accessories included with the buses.

🔍

Inspection Certificate

Third-party or in-house inspection report confirming the condition and specifications of each bus before shipping.

🌍

Customs Assistance

We assist with customs documentation and can advise on import requirements for your specific destination country.
